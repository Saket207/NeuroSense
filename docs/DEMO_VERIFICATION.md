## 1. Executive Summary

The **NeuroSense Demo Studio** provides a transparent, auditable demonstration harness for hackathon evaluators and clinical judges. It enables one-click autofill of realistic fictional elder profiles, automated streaming of synthesized speech audio into the live speech-to-text and feature analysis pipeline, and real-time visualization of intermediate calculations inside the **Pipeline Inspector**.

### Non-Negotiable Rules Adherence
- **Inputs Only:** Demo scenarios supply form values and audio files only. Transcripts, match decisions, stage credits, acoustic biometrics, CSI scores, risk tiers, and confidence levels are **100% computed live by the real pipeline**.
- **Zero Mock Logic:** No mock, hardcoded, or random values exist in any scoring or inference code path.
- **Strict Codebase Isolation:** Core production modules (`life_memory_matcher.py`, `unified_scorer.py`, `screening_engine.py`, `audio_analyzer.py`, `speech_features.py`, `src/services/report.ts`) have zero references to persona names or scenario IDs (verified by automated scanner).
- **Clinical Safety Disclaimer:** Mandatory non-diagnostic disclaimer is rendered on all demo screens, inspector views, and generated clinical reports.
